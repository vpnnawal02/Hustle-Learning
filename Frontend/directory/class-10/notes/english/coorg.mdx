## Summary

In the chapter Coorg, the author describes Coorg, also known as Kodagu, as a beautiful region of Karnataka situated between Mysore and Mangalore. Famous for its rolling hills, evergreen rainforests, spices and coffee plantations, Coorg is considered a heavenly destination for nature lovers. The best time to visit is from September to March, when the weather is pleasant and the air is filled with the refreshing aroma of coffee.

The people of Coorg are known for their bravery, independence and hospitality. Their origin is believed to be either Greek or Arabic, and their distinctive martial traditions, marriage customs and traditional dress support these theories. The Coorg Regiment is one of the most decorated regiments in the Indian Army, and General Cariappa, the first Indian Chief of the Indian Army, belonged to Coorg.

The region is also famous for its natural beauty and rich wildlife. The River Kaveri originates in the hills and forests of Coorg and supports a variety of aquatic life, including the mahaseer fish. Elephants, kingfishers, squirrels, langurs and other animals can be spotted in the region. Coorg offers numerous adventure activities, such as river rafting, canoeing, rappelling, rock climbing, mountain biking and trekking. Visitors can enjoy breathtaking views from the Brahmagiri hills and explore the island of Nisargadhama through a rope bridge.

Another major attraction is Bylakuppe, home to India's largest Tibetan settlement, where Buddhist monks in colourful robes add to the region's cultural diversity. Overall, Coorg is a unique destination that combines natural beauty, adventure, wildlife, cultural richness and a proud martial heritage.

## Frequently asked questions

Q: Why is Coorg called a heavenly destination?

A: Rolling hills, evergreen rainforests, coffee aroma, pleasant September-to-March weather and rich wildlife make it a paradise for nature lovers.

Q: What proves the martial heritage of Coorg?

A: The highly decorated Coorg Regiment and General Cariappa, the first Indian Army Chief, both belong to Coorg.
