One-paragraph revision of His First Flight by Liam O' Flaherty: fear lives in the mind until one leap proves the wings work.

## Summary

The story is about a young seagull who is afraid to fly. All his younger siblings despite their much shorter wings flew fearlessly while he could not gather the courage to trust his own wings. The young seagull became sad when he saw his parents perfecting his younger siblings in the art of flying. His parents scold and taunt him for not even trying. They even call him a coward. They tell him a lot to at least try and also threaten him to leave him alone and hungry if he does not.

The following day, he is left in isolation and upon feeling hungry, he tries to seek the attention of his family members. Only his mother, who is tearing a fish in an attempt to eat it, notices his son. The young seagull cries out of starvation hoping his mother would help. On seeing his mother come to him with the fish, he gets excited. But on noticing that his mother stopped mid-way, he gets maddened out of hunger and takes a dive at the fish, forgetting for a moment that he is afraid to fly.

Finally, he took his first flight. All the family members celebrate his victory by cheering and dancing around him. He also attempts floating in the sea that he was once afraid of. Thus, he overcomes his fear and realizes that it is all in the mind.

```keys
- Fear kept the seagull on the ledge; hunger stronger than fear pushed him into the air, where his wings proved themselves.
- The family's celebration marks his passage from dependence and self-doubt to confidence and independence.
```

## Frequently asked questions

Q: Why could the young seagull not fly at first?

A: He was convinced his wings would not support him, so despite watching his siblings succeed, terror kept him on the ledge.

Q: What finally makes him take his first flight?

A: Maddened by hunger, he dives at the fish his mother holds just out of reach, falls into the air, and discovers he can soar.
