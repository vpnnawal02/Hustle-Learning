One-paragraph revision of The Book That Saved the Earth by Claire Boiko: how nursery rhymes cancelled a Martian invasion.

## Summary

The play is a humorous science-fiction story set in the twenty-fifth century. It describes how a simple book of nursery rhymes, Mother Goose, accidentally saves Earth from a Martian invasion in the year 2040. The story begins with a Historian at the Museum of Ancient History explaining that the twentieth century was known as the Era of the Book because books were available on almost every subject and served as important sources of knowledge. She then narrates the events of the Martian invasion that never took place.

The Martians, led by the arrogant and self-proclaimed intelligent Think-Tank, plan to invade Earth, which they consider a primitive planet. Think-Tank sends a space crew consisting of Captain Omega, Lieutenant Iota and Sergeant Oop to Earth to gather information. The crew enters a public library in Centerville but fails to understand the purpose of the books. Think-Tank initially mistakes the library for a refreshment stand and assumes that books are sandwiches eaten by Earthlings. He orders the crew to eat a book, and Sergeant Oop reluctantly bites a corner of one, finding it dry and unpleasant.

When Apprentice Noodle suggests that Earthlings use books for communication, Think-Tank declares that they are communication devices. He first orders the crew to listen to the books, but when they hear nothing, he changes his explanation and claims that books are meant for communication through the eyes. The crew then examines a copy of Mother Goose, which contains nursery rhymes. Think-Tank misinterprets the rhymes as evidence of Earth's extraordinary scientific and military capabilities.

When he hears Mistress Mary, Quite Contrary, he assumes that Earthlings can grow metals such as silver and even explosives through agriculture. The rhyme Hey Diddle Diddle convinces him that Earthlings have trained animals to perform musical activities and master space techniques, leading him to fear an attack by millions of cows. Finally, when Sergeant Oop reads Humpty Dumpty, Think-Tank notices that the illustrated character resembles his own large, egg-shaped head. He interprets the line about Humpty Dumpty's great fall as a warning that Earthlings intend to capture him and overthrow the Martian leadership.

Terrified, Think-Tank immediately cancels the invasion, orders the Martians to evacuate Mars and escapes to Alpha Centauri. His intelligent assistant, Noodle, later becomes the ruler of Mars. The Historian concludes that Earth and Mars eventually establish friendly relations, and the Martians learn to read and understand books. However, they remain reluctant to read Mother Goose because of its role in frightening Think-Tank and preventing their invasion.

```keys
- The play highlights the importance of books and knowledge while using humour and dramatic irony to expose arrogance, ignorance and the dangers of making decisions based on incorrect information.
- It also demonstrates how a simple misunderstanding can change the course of history.
```

## Frequently asked questions

Q: How does Mother Goose save the Earth?

A: Think-Tank reads its rhymes as proof of Earth's super-weapons, panics at Humpty Dumpty, and calls off the invasion.

Q: Who becomes the ruler of Mars after Think-Tank flees?

A: Noodle, the humble, sensible apprentice whose quiet corrections proved wiser than his master's boasts.
