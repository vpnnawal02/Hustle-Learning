One-paragraph revision of A Triumph of Surgery by James Herriot: how overpampering made Tricki ill and simple care cured him.

## Summary

The story highlights the harmful effects of excessive pampering and overindulgence through the story of a small dog named Tricki and his wealthy mistress, Mrs Pumphrey. Tricki becomes extremely fat, lazy and unhealthy because Mrs Pumphrey feeds him rich food, cream cakes, chocolates, malt, cod-liver oil and Horlicks, believing that he is suffering from malnutrition.

Despite the veterinary surgeon Mr James Herriot's advice to reduce Tricki's food and increase his exercise, Mrs Pumphrey fails to follow his instructions because she cannot bear to refuse her beloved pet anything. As a result, Tricki's condition deteriorates, and he becomes listless, refuses food and suffers from vomiting. Concerned about his health, Mr Herriot decides to hospitalise him for a fortnight.

At the veterinary surgery, Tricki initially receives no food but plenty of water. Gradually, he begins to recover as he interacts with other dogs, runs around the garden and develops healthy eating habits. He receives no medicines or surgical treatment; his recovery is brought about by a balanced diet, regular physical activity and the company of other dogs.

Meanwhile, Mrs Pumphrey remains anxious and frequently calls to enquire about his condition. When she learns that Tricki is recovering, she sends fresh eggs, wine and brandy to help restore his strength. However, these provisions are enjoyed by Mr Herriot and his colleagues rather than Tricki. After two weeks, Tricki becomes active, muscular and healthy. When Mrs Pumphrey arrives to collect him, she is delighted to see his remarkable transformation and expresses her gratitude to Mr Herriot, calling the incident a triumph of surgery.

```keys
- The title is ironic because Tricki does not undergo any surgery; he recovers through proper care, a controlled diet and exercise.
- Excessive love and indulgence can be harmful, and genuine care requires discipline, responsibility and an understanding of what is truly beneficial for those we love.
```

## Frequently asked questions

Q: What made Tricki ill in A Triumph of Surgery?

A: Overfeeding with rich food and treats plus almost no exercise made him obese, lethargic and seriously unwell.

Q: Why is the title A Triumph of Surgery ironic?

A: No surgery or medicine is used at all; Tricki recovers through diet control, water, exercise and play with other dogs.
