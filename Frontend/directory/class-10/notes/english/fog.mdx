## Stanza

```stanza
The fog comes
on little cat feet
It sits looking
over harbour and city
on silent haunches
and then moves on
```

**Line 1: "The fog comes on little cat feet."**

**Explanation:** The poet compares the arrival of fog to the silent movement of a cat. Just as a cat walks softly and quietly without making any noise, the fog also arrives silently and gradually. The poet uses a metaphor to compare the fog to a cat.

**Literary Device:** Metaphor

**Line 2: "It sits looking over harbour and city"**

**Explanation:** The poet imagines the fog as a cat that sits quietly and observes its surroundings. The fog covers the harbour (a place where ships are kept) and the city, making them appear unclear and misty. The poet describes the fog as if it were a living creature that can sit and look around.

**Literary Device:** Personification

**Line 3: "on silent haunches"**

**Explanation:** Haunches refers to the back legs and hips of an animal, especially when it is sitting. The poet compares the fog to a cat sitting quietly on its hind legs. Just as a cat sits silently and observes its surroundings, the fog remains still and silent over the harbour and city.

**Literary Device:** Metaphor and imagery

**Line 4: "and then moves on."**

**Explanation:** Just as a cat quietly leaves a place after sitting there for some time, the fog also gradually disappears and moves away. It does not remain in one place forever.

**Literary Device:** Metaphor
