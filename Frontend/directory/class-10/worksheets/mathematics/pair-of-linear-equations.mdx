Three timed practice worksheets for Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables. Attempt one worksheet at a time on paper, then tap each question to check its answer.

> [!TIP] Attempt the 1-mark questions in under a minute each; save written solutions for the 3 and 4-mark questions.

## Worksheet 1

Q: How many solutions does a pair of coincident linear equations have? (1 mark)

A: Infinitely many solutions.

Q: What is the condition for a pair of linear equations to have no solution? (1 mark)

A: a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂.

Q: If two straight lines intersect at exactly one point, how many solutions does the corresponding pair have? (1 mark)

A: One solution.

Q: What is the geometrical interpretation of a pair of inconsistent linear equations? (1 mark)

A: Two distinct parallel lines.

Q: When a polynomial of degree 4 is divided by a quadratic polynomial, what is the maximum possible degree of the remainder? (1 mark)

A: 1.

Q: If a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂, what is the nature of the pair of linear equations? (1 mark)

A: Parallel lines with no solution.

Q: Solve 2x − 3y = 5 and x + y = 1 using the substitution method. (2 marks)

A: x = 8/5 and y = −3/5.

Q: Solve 4x + 3y = 14 and 2x + 5y = 12 using the elimination method. (2 marks)

A: x = 17/7 and y = 10/7.

Q: Without drawing graphs, determine whether 7x + 2y − 9 = 0 and 21x + 6y − 27 = 0 are intersecting, parallel or coincident. (2 marks)

A: Coincident lines.

Q: 4 pencils and 6 pens cost ₹44, while 6 pencils and 4 pens cost ₹36. Find the cost of one pencil and one pen. (2 marks)

A: Pencil = ₹2 and pen = ₹6.

Q: Solve 97x + 103y = 497 and 103x + 97y = 503. (2 marks)

A: x = 3 and y = 2.

Q: Solve x + 2y = 7 and 3x − y = 5 graphically. (3 marks)

A: x = 17/7 and y = 16/7.

Q: Solve x/12 + y/6 = 7/2 and x/9 + y/6 = 3 using elimination. (3 marks)

A: x = −18 and y = 30.

Q: A box has only 10-paise and 20-paise coins. There are 60 coins worth ₹9 in total. Find the number of each type. (3 marks)

A: 30 coins of 10 paise and 30 coins of 20 paise.

Q: A two-digit number has its units digit three times its tens digit. Adding 18 interchanges the digits. Find the number. (3 marks)

A: 13.

Q: Draw the graphs of 3x + y = 9 and 3x − y + 3 = 0. Shade the region bounded by these lines and the x-axis. Find its area. (4 marks)

A: 12 sq units.

Q: A boat travels 36 km upstream and 48 km downstream in 10 hours. On another journey it covers 42 km upstream and 30 km downstream in 11 hours. Find the speeds of the boat and the current. (4 marks)

A: Boat = 819/38 km/h (about 21.6) and current = 663/38 km/h (about 17.4).

Q: A shop sells 5 chairs and 4 tables for ₹2,600, while 3 chairs and 5 tables cost ₹2,850. Find each price separately. (4 marks)

A: Chair = ₹1600/13 (about ₹123.08) and table = ₹5160/13 (about ₹396.92).

## Worksheet 2

Q: How many solutions does a pair of coincident linear equations have? (1 mark)

A: Infinitely many solutions.

Q: What is the condition for a pair of linear equations to have no solution? (1 mark)

A: a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂.

Q: What is the condition for a pair of linear equations to have a unique solution? (1 mark)

A: a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂.

Q: What is the geometrical interpretation of an inconsistent pair of linear equations? (1 mark)

A: Two distinct parallel lines.

Q: If two straight lines intersect at exactly one point, what can you say about the corresponding pair? (1 mark)

A: They have a unique solution.

Q: How many different pairs of linear equations can have the same unique solution x = 2, y = −4? (1 mark)

A: Infinitely many pairs.

Q: Solve 4x + 3y = 14 and 2x + 5y = 12 using the elimination method. (2 marks)

A: x = 17/7 and y = 10/7.

Q: Solve 7x + 4y = 13 and 3x + 2y = 7. (2 marks)

A: x = −1 and y = 5.

Q: A taxi charges a fixed fee plus a per-km charge. 15 km costs ₹95 and 25 km costs ₹145. Find both charges. (2 marks)

A: Fixed charge = ₹20 and charge per km = ₹5.

Q: Two supplementary angles differ by 24°. Find the angles. (2 marks)

A: 102° and 78°.

Q: A box has only 10-paise and 20-paise coins. There are 60 coins worth ₹9 in total. Find the number of each type. (2 marks)

A: 30 coins of 10 paise and 30 coins of 20 paise.

Q: Solve x + 2y = 7 and 3x − y = 5 graphically. (3 marks)

A: x = 17/7 and y = 16/7.

Q: Show graphically that 3x + 6y = 12 and x + 2y = 7 has no solution. (3 marks)

A: No solution. The two lines are parallel and distinct.

Q: Solve x − 2y = 3 and 3x + y = 11. (3 marks)

A: x = 25/7 and y = 2/7.

Q: The sum of two numbers is 900 and the difference of their squares is 18,000. Find the numbers. (3 marks)

A: 460 and 440.

Q: Five years ago a father was four times as old as his son. Three years from now he will be twice as old. Find their present ages. (4 marks)

A: Father = 21 years and son = 9 years.

Q: Two cyclists 24 km apart move towards each other and meet after 2 hours. Moving the same direction, the faster catches the slower after 8 hours. Find their speeds. (4 marks)

A: Faster cyclist = 7.5 km/h and slower cyclist = 4.5 km/h.

Q: Student A takes hostel meals for 18 days and pays ₹920; student B takes 25 days and pays ₹1,200. Find the fixed charge and daily food charge. (4 marks)

A: Fixed charge = ₹200 and daily food charge = ₹40.

## Worksheet 3

Q: What is the geometrical interpretation of coincident linear equations? (1 mark)

A: Two coincident straight lines having infinitely many common points.

Q: What is the condition for infinitely many solutions? (1 mark)

A: a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ = c₁/c₂.

Q: When a degree-5 polynomial is divided by a degree-2 polynomial, what is the maximum possible degree of the remainder? (1 mark)

A: 1.

Q: How many linear equations can pass through a given point? (1 mark)

A: Infinitely many.

Q: If two lines are parallel, how many points of intersection do they have? (1 mark)

A: Zero.

Q: If the graph of ax + by + c = 0 intersects the y-axis below the origin, what is the sign of c? (1 mark)

A: Negative (c < 0).

Q: Solve 2x − 3y = 5 and x + y = 1 using the substitution method. (2 marks)

A: x = 8/5 and y = −3/5.

Q: Solve 0.4x + 0.6y = 0.68 and 0.2x + 0.5y = 0.46. (2 marks)

A: x = 0.8 and y = 0.6.

Q: Without drawing graphs, determine whether 5x − 4y + 8 = 0 and 10x − 8y + 3 = 0 are intersecting, parallel or coincident. (2 marks)

A: Parallel lines.

Q: A says to B: give me 12 oranges and I will have twice as many as you. B replies: give me 12 and we will be equal. Find each friend's oranges. (2 marks)

A: A = 84 oranges and B = 60 oranges.

Q: A library charges a fixed amount for the first 4 days plus a daily extra after that. Aarav paid ₹30 for 8 days and Rohan ₹22 for 6 days. Find both charges. (2 marks)

A: Fixed charge = ₹14 and extra charge = ₹4 per day.

Q: Solve 23x + 41y = 105 and 41x + 23y = 159 using elimination. (3 marks)

A: x = 57/16 and y = 9/16.

Q: Solve 97x + 103y = 497 and 103x + 97y = 503. (3 marks)

A: x = 3 and y = 2.

Q: A person says: give me ₹80 and I will have three times your money. The friend replies: give me ₹40 and I will have twice yours. Find each person's money. (3 marks)

A: First person = ₹136 and second person = ₹152.

Q: Meena withdraws ₹2,400 in ₹50 and ₹100 notes only, receiving 30 notes. Find the number of each. (3 marks)

A: Twelve ₹50 notes and eighteen ₹100 notes.

Q: Shifting 12 candidates from room A to B makes A half of B; shifting 8 from B to A makes A three times B. Find the candidates in each room. (4 marks)

A: Room A = 28 and Room B = 20.

Q: A person rows at 7 km/h in still water. Rowing 42 km upstream takes twice as long as downstream. Find the stream's speed. (4 marks)

A: 7/3 km/h, that is, 2⅓ km/h.

Q: A two-digit number has digits with product 24. Adding 18 interchanges the digits. Find the number. (4 marks)

A: 46.

```keys
- Consistency: a₁/a₂ vs b₁/b₂ vs c₁/c₂ decides intersecting, parallel or coincident lines.
- Methods: substitution, elimination and graphs all lead to the same pair solution.
- Word problems: coins, ages, speeds, charges and digits all translate into pairs of equations.
```
