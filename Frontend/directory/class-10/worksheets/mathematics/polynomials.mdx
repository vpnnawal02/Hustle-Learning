## Worksheet 2

Q: What is meant by a polynomial with real coefficients? (1 mark)

A: A polynomial whose coefficients are all real numbers.

Q: Define a zero of a polynomial. (1 mark)

A: A number k is called a zero of a polynomial p(x) if p(k) = 0.

Q: What is meant by the value of a polynomial at a particular point? (1 mark)

A: The value obtained by substituting the given point for the variable in the polynomial.

Q: If the graph of ax² + bx + c intersects the positive side of the y-axis, what can you say about the sign of c? (1 mark)

A: c > 0.

Q: If a quadratic polynomial is the square of a linear polynomial, its two zeroes are equal. State True or False. (1 mark)

A: True.

Q: If a quadratic polynomial can be expressed as a product of two distinct linear factors, how many real and distinct zeroes does it have? (1 mark)

A: Two real and distinct zeroes.

Q: Construct a quadratic polynomial whose sum and product of zeroes are 0 and −7 respectively. (2 marks)

A: x² − 7.

Q: If one zero of x² − 7x + k is 3, find the value of k. (2 marks)

A: k = 12.

Q: For what value of k is 4 a zero of 3x² − 2x + k? (2 marks)

A: k = −40.

Q: If −2 is a zero of x² + 5x + k, find the value of k. (2 marks)

A: k = −6.

Q: If α and β are the zeroes of x² − 6x + 5, find the value of α² + β². (2 marks)

A: 26.

Q: Find the zeroes of 9x² − 12x + 4 and verify the relationship between the zeroes and its coefficients. (3 marks)

A: α = 2/3 and β = 2/3. Sum = 4/3 = −(−12)/9 and product = 4/9, which match −b/a and c/a.

Q: If α and β are the zeroes of 5x² + x − 6, find α/β + β/α. (3 marks)

A: −61/30.

Q: If α and β are the zeroes of x² − 3x + 1, find α²β + αβ². (3 marks)

A: 3.

Q: The sum of the squares of the zeroes of x² − 8x + k is 40. Find k. (3 marks)

A: k = 12.

Q: If α and β are the zeroes of 3x² − 10x + 4, form a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are 2α and 2β. (4 marks)

A: 3x² − 20x + 16.

Q: If the zeroes of x² + px + 12 differ by 2, find the possible values of p. (4 marks)

A: p = 2√13 or p = −2√13.

Q: If α and β are the zeroes of f(x) = kx² + 6x + 3 and α² + β² = 18, find the possible values of k. (4 marks)

A: k = (−1 + √73)/6 or k = (−1 − √73)/6.

## Worksheet 3

Q: Define the degree of a polynomial. (1 mark)

A: The highest power of the variable with a non-zero coefficient.

Q: Write the general form of a quadratic polynomial with real coefficients. (1 mark)

A: ax² + bx + c, where a ≠ 0 and a, b, c are real numbers.

Q: If a quadratic polynomial cannot be factorised into linear factors over the real numbers, then it has no real zeroes. State True or False. (1 mark)

A: True.

Q: If the graph of ax² + bx + c intersects the negative side of the y-axis, what can you say about the sign of c? (1 mark)

A: c < 0.

Q: If f(a) and f(b) have opposite signs for a polynomial f(x), what is the minimum number of zeroes between a and b? (1 mark)

A: At least one real zero.

Q: If the zeroes of a quadratic polynomial are equal, state the value of its discriminant and the condition. (1 mark)

A: Discriminant = 0, that is, b² − 4ac = 0.

Q: If the product of the zeroes of ax² − 8x − 12 is −6, find a. (2 marks)

A: a = 2.

Q: If α and β are the zeroes of 2x² − 7x + 3, find α + β and αβ. (2 marks)

A: α + β = 7/2 and αβ = 3/2.

Q: The product of the zeroes of f(x) = x² − 4x + k is 5. Find k. (2 marks)

A: k = 5.

Q: If (x + a) is a factor of 2x² + 2ax + 5x + 10, find a. (2 marks)

A: a = 2.

Q: If the product of the zeroes of 5x² − 8x + k is 3, find k. (2 marks)

A: k = 15.

Q: If α and β are the zeroes of 3x² + 4x + k and α² + β² + αβ = 13/9, find k. (3 marks)

A: k = 1.

Q: If α and β are the zeroes of x² − 6x + k and α − β = 2, find k. (3 marks)

A: k = 8.

Q: If the sum of the squares of the zeroes of x² − 10x + k is 52, find k. (3 marks)

A: k = 24.

Q: If α and β are the zeroes of p(x) = 3x² − 8x + 2, find α² + β². (3 marks)

A: 52/9.

Q: If α and β are the zeroes of x² − 4x + 3, form a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are 3α + 2 and 3β + 2. (4 marks)

A: x² − 16x + 55.

Q: If α and β are the zeroes of 3x² − 7x + 5, form a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are 2α + 3β and 3α + 2β. (4 marks)

A: 3x² − 35x + 103.

Q: If x³ + ax² + bx + 6 is divisible by x + 2 and x − 1, find a and b. (4 marks)

A: a = −2 and b = −5.
