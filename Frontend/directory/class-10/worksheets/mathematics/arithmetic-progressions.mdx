Two timed practice worksheets for Arithmetic Progressions. Attempt one worksheet at a time on paper, then tap each question to check its answer.

> [!TIP] Attempt the 1-mark questions in under a minute each; save written solutions for the 3, 4 and 6-mark questions.

## Worksheet 1

Q: The nth term of an A.P. is 7n − 3. Find its common difference. (1 mark)

A: 7.

Q: Find the first term and common difference of the A.P. 2, 7, 12, 17, .... (1 mark)

A: First term = 2 and common difference = 5.

Q: If the common difference of an A.P. is zero, what can you say about its terms? (1 mark)

A: All the terms are equal.

Q: Determine whether the sequence 2n² + 3 forms an A.P. (1 mark)

A: No. Successive differences are 5, 9, 13, which are not constant.

Q: Write the formula for the nth term of an A.P. (1 mark)

A: aₙ = a + (n − 1)d.

Q: How many two-digit numbers are divisible by 6? (1 mark)

A: 15.

Q: Write the first six terms of an A.P. whose first term is 12 and common difference is 4. (2 marks)

A: 12, 16, 20, 24, 28 and 32.

Q: Find the common difference and write the next four terms of the A.P. 6, 1, −4, −9, .... (2 marks)

A: Common difference = −5. Next terms: −14, −19, −24 and −29.

Q: Which term of the A.P. 4, 9, 14, 19, ... is 154? (2 marks)

A: The 31st term.

Q: The 8th term of an A.P. is 35 and its 14th term is 59. Find the first term and common difference. (2 marks)

A: First term = 7 and common difference = 4.

Q: Determine whether 1.2, 1.8, 2.4, 3.0, ... is an A.P. If yes, find its common difference. (2 marks)

A: Yes. Common difference = 0.6.

Q: Find the 10th, 18th and nth terms of the A.P. 7, 12, 17, 22, 27, .... (3 marks)

A: 52, 92 and 5n + 2.

Q: A student saves ₹200 in the first month and increases savings by ₹50 every month. Write the first six months' sequence. Is it an A.P.? If yes, find its common difference. (3 marks)

A: 200, 250, 300, 350, 400, 450. Yes, with common difference 50.

Q: Find the number of three-digit natural numbers divisible by 9. (3 marks)

A: 100.

Q: The 5th term of an A.P. is 18 and the 13th term is 50. Find its 21st term. (3 marks)

A: 82.

Q: Determine the 12th term from the end of the A.P. 5, 9, 13, 17, ..., 201. (4 marks)

A: 157.

Q: Find the sum of the first 25 terms of the A.P. 3, 8, 13, 18, .... (4 marks)

A: 1575.

Q: The sum of the first n terms of an A.P. is Sₙ = 4n² + 5n. Find its nth term and its 18th term. (4 marks)

A: nth term = 8n + 1 and 18th term = 145.

Q: Meera wants to save ₹7,200 in 12 months. She saves ₹500 in the first month and increases savings by ₹25 every month. How much will she save? Will she achieve her target? (4 marks)

A: ₹7,650. Yes, she exceeds the target.

Q: The sum of the first 10 terms of an A.P. is 145 and the sum of the first 20 terms is 490. Find the first term, common difference and the sum of the first 30 terms. (6 marks)

A: First term = 11/2, common difference = 2, and S₃₀ = 1035.

## Worksheet 2

Q: Find the common difference of the A.P. whose nth term is 5n + 12. (1 mark)

A: 5.

Q: Write the first term and common difference of the A.P. 15, 11, 7, 3, .... (1 mark)

A: First term = 15 and common difference = −4.

Q: Determine whether the sequence aₙ = n² + 2 forms an A.P. (1 mark)

A: No. Successive differences are 3, 5, 7, which are not constant.

Q: Find the common difference of the A.P. 9, 3, −3, −9, .... (1 mark)

A: −6.

Q: If n + 1, 3n − 2 and 7n + 5 are in A.P., find n. (1 mark)

A: n = −5.

Q: Find the first positive term of the A.P. −35, −29, −23, −17, .... (1 mark)

A: 1, which is the 7th term.

Q: Find the common difference and write the next four terms of the A.P. −3, 2, 7, 12, .... (2 marks)

A: Common difference = 5. Next terms: 17, 22, 27 and 32.

Q: Which term of the A.P. 4, 10, 16, 22, ... will be 132 more than its 18th term? (2 marks)

A: The 40th term.

Q: The 7th term of an A.P. is 29 and the 15th term is 61. Find the first term and common difference. (2 marks)

A: First term = 5 and common difference = 4.

Q: An employee earns ₹60,000 in the first year with a ₹4,000 annual increment. In which year will his salary become ₹88,000? (2 marks)

A: In the 8th year.

Q: Find the 10th term from the end of the A.P. 3, 8, 13, ..., 198. (2 marks)

A: 153.

Q: Find the number of terms in the A.P. 12, 17, 22, 27, ..., 207. (3 marks)

A: 40 terms.

Q: Find the number of all three-digit natural numbers divisible by 8. (3 marks)

A: 112.

Q: Find the sum of the first 30 terms of the A.P. 6, 10, 14, 18, .... (3 marks)

A: 1920.

Q: Find the sum of all multiples of 7 between 50 and 500. (3 marks)

A: 17696.

Q: The 4th term, 12th term and last term of an A.P. are 2, 26 and 98. Find the common difference and the number of terms. (4 marks)

A: Common difference = 3 and number of terms = 36.

Q: Find the A.P. whose 4th term is 18 and whose 8th term is 34. (4 marks)

A: 6, 10, 14, 18, ....

Q: Find the sum of the first 18 terms of an A.P. in which the 4th term is 15 and the 9th term is 35. (4 marks)

A: 666.

Q: A person saved ₹18,000 in 10 years, saving ₹150 more each year than the preceding year. How much did he save in the first year? (4 marks)

A: ₹1,125.

Q: A thief runs at 45 m/min. After 3 minutes a policeman starts chasing at 55 m in the first minute, increasing speed by 5 m/min every minute. After how many minutes will he catch the thief? (6 marks)

A: After 6 minutes of chasing.

```keys
- nth term: aₙ = a + (n − 1)d locates terms, counts and first positives.
- Sums: Sₙ = n/2 × (2a + (n − 1)d) totals savings, multiples and long additions.
- Applications: salaries, savings plans and chase problems all reduce to AP equations.
```
