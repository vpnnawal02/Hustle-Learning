Two timed practice worksheets for Coordinate Geometry. Attempt one worksheet at a time on paper, then tap each question to check its answer.

## Worksheet 1

Q: Identify the positions of P(7, 0) and Q(0, −3). (1 mark)

Q: Find the distance between (3, 4) and (3, 10). (1 mark)

Q: Find the midpoint of the line segment joining (2, 4) and (6, 8). (1 mark)

Q: Find the distance between (a, 0) and (0, b). (1 mark)

Q: Find the centroid of the triangle with vertices (3, 3), (6, 6) and (9, 3). (1 mark)

Q: Find the value of a when the distance between (3, 4) and (a, 10) is 5 units. (2 marks)

Q: Find a point on the y-axis equidistant from A(5, −2) and B(−3, 2). (2 marks)

Q: Find the point dividing the segment joining A(2, 5) and B(8, −1) in the ratio 2:1 internally. (2 marks)

Q: Find the coordinates of the point which divides the segment joining A(1, −3) and B(7, 6) into three equal parts. (2 marks)

Q: Find the point where the diagonals of the parallelogram with vertices (−4, −2), (2, 1), (6, 5) and (0, 2) meet. (2 marks)

Q: Determine whether A(3, 2), B(−2, −3) and C(2, 3) form a triangle. If they do, state whether it is scalene, isosceles or equilateral. (3 marks)

Q: Check whether the points A(2, −1), B(5, 2) and C(9, 5) are collinear. (3 marks)

Q: Find the point dividing the segment joining A(5, 2) and B(3, −4) in the ratio 2:1 internally. (3 marks)

Q: Find the centroid of the triangle with vertices (1, 5), (4, −2) and (−3, 1). (3 marks)

Q: Find the coordinates of the points which trisect the segment joining A(1, −3) and B(7, 6). (3 marks)

Q: Show that P(2, −1), Q(3, 4), R(−2, 3) and S(−3, −2) form a rhombus. Also find its area. (4 marks)

Q: Prove that A(−3, 0), B(1, −3) and C(4, 1) are vertices of an isosceles right-angled triangle. Also find its area. (4 marks)

Q: Find the circumcentre of the triangle with vertices (7, 5), (9, −1) and (3, −3). Also find its circumradius. (4 marks)

Q: The points (1, 5), (7, 1), (5, −3) and (−1, 1), taken in order, form a quadrilateral. Identify its type and justify. (4 marks)

Q: A(4, 2), B(6, 5) and C(2, 4) are vertices of triangle ABC. Find the midpoint D of BC, the point P on AD with AP:PD = 2:1, and state what P is. (4 marks)

## Worksheet 2

Q: Find the distance between (0, 0) and (3, 4). (1 mark)

Q: Find the midpoint of the line segment joining (2, 4) and (6, 8). (1 mark)

Q: Find the centroid of the triangle whose vertices are (3, 3), (6, 6) and (9, 3). (1 mark)

Q: Find the distance between (4, 0) and (0, 3). (1 mark)

Q: Find the coordinates of the midpoint of the segment joining (−2, 4) and (2, −4). (1 mark)

Q: A line segment is 10 units long. One endpoint is (2, 4) and the abscissa of the other endpoint is 10. Find its ordinate. (2 marks)

Q: Find a point on the y-axis equidistant from A(5, −2) and B(−3, 2). (2 marks)

Q: Find the point dividing the segment joining A(2, 5) and B(8, −1) in the ratio 2:1 internally. (2 marks)

Q: One endpoint of a diameter with centre C(2, −3) is A(4, −1). Find the other endpoint. (2 marks)

Q: Determine whether A(2, 4), B(6, 5) and C(9, 8) are collinear. (2 marks)

Q: Find y for which the distance between P(2, −3) and Q(10, y) is 12 units. (3 marks)

Q: Find the coordinates of the points which trisect the segment joining A(1, −3) and B(7, 6). (3 marks)

Q: If A(3, y) is equidistant from P(5, −2) and Q(7, 6), find y and the distance AQ. (3 marks)

Q: Find the equation of the perpendicular bisector of the segment joining (6, 2) and (3, 5). (3 marks)

Q: If C(−1, 3) divides the segment joining A(4, 5) and B in the ratio 2:3 internally, find B. (3 marks)

Q: Prove that A(2, −1), B(4, 6), C(10, 8) and D(8, 1) are vertices of a parallelogram. (4 marks)

Q: Prove that (3, −3), (3, 2) and (7, 2) are vertices of a right-angled triangle. Also find its area and hypotenuse. (4 marks)

Q: ABCD is a rectangle with A(−2, −1), B(−2, 5), C(6, 5) and D(6, −1). If P, Q, R, S are midpoints of AB, BC, CD, DA, determine whether PQRS is a square, rectangle or rhombus. (4 marks)

Q: Prove that (−5, −2), (−1, 3), (4, −1) and (0, −6) are vertices of a rectangle. (4 marks)

Q: The vertices of a triangle are (4, 5), (5, 7) and (7, 8). Find its centroid and verify that the centroid divides each median in the ratio 2:1. (4 marks)
