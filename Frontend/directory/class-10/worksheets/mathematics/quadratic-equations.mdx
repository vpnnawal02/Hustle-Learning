Three timed practice worksheets for Quadratic Equations. Attempt one worksheet at a time on paper, then tap each question to check its answer.

> [!TIP] Attempt the 1-mark questions in under a minute each; save written solutions for the 3 and 4-mark questions.

## Worksheet 1

Q: Find the discriminant of 2x² − 8x + 7 = 0. (1 mark)

A: D = 8.

Q: Determine the nature of the roots of 5x² − 6√3x + 4 = 0. (1 mark)

A: Two distinct real roots.

Q: What is the condition for a quadratic equation to have no real roots? (1 mark)

A: D < 0.

Q: Find the roots of x² + 6x + 9 = 0. (1 mark)

A: x = −3 and −3.

Q: Find the discriminant of 3x² + 2√5x − 5 = 0. (1 mark)

A: D = 80.

Q: If the discriminant of a quadratic equation is positive, what is the nature of its roots? (1 mark)

A: Two distinct real roots.

Q: Solve 25x(x + 2) = 6. (2 marks)

A: x = −1 + √31/5 or x = −1 − √31/5.

Q: Determine whether 3x² + 2√5x − 5 = 0 has real roots. If yes, find them. (2 marks)

A: Yes. x = √5/3 or x = −√5.

Q: Find the roots of (x − 5)(x − 6) = 625/16. (2 marks)

A: x = (22 + √629)/4 or x = (22 − √629)/4.

Q: The product of two consecutive positive integers is 240. Form a quadratic equation to determine the integers. (2 marks)

A: x² + x − 240 = 0. The integers are 15 and 16.

Q: Find the values of k for which 3x² − 2(k + 2)x + (k + 2) = 0 has equal roots. (2 marks)

A: k = 1 or k = −2.

Q: Solve 1/(x − 2) − 1/(x + 4) = 3/5, where x ≠ 2, −4. (3 marks)

A: x = −1 + √19 or x = −1 − √19.

Q: The sum of the squares of two consecutive positive integers is 365. Find the integers. (3 marks)

A: 13 and 14.

Q: Solve x + 4/x = 5, where x ≠ 0. (3 marks)

A: x = 1 or x = 4.

Q: The sum of the squares of two consecutive even positive integers is 452. Find the integers. (3 marks)

A: 14 and 16.

Q: The perimeter of a rectangular field is 86 m and its area is 420 m². Find its length and breadth. (4 marks)

A: Length = 28 m and breadth = 15 m.

Q: The area of a rectangular plot is 576 m². Its length is 4 m more than twice its breadth. Find the length and breadth. (4 marks)

A: Length = 36 m and breadth = 16 m.

Q: A cottage industry produces pottery articles each day. The cost per article is ₹4 more than twice the number of articles produced that day. If the total production cost is ₹96, find the number of articles and the cost of each article. (4 marks)

A: 6 articles at ₹16 each.

## Worksheet 2

Q: What is the condition for a quadratic equation to have two distinct real roots? (1 mark)

A: D > 0.

Q: Find the discriminant of x² − 4x + k = 0. (1 mark)

A: D = 16 − 4k.

Q: Determine the nature of the roots of 3x² − 4x + 2 = 0. (1 mark)

A: No real roots.

Q: If the discriminant of a quadratic equation is zero, what is the nature of its roots? (1 mark)

A: Two equal and real roots.

Q: Write the standard form of a quadratic equation. (1 mark)

A: ax² + bx + c = 0, where a ≠ 0.

Q: Find the discriminant of 5x² + 7x − 3 = 0. (1 mark)

A: 109.

Q: Solve x² − 3abx + 2b² = 0. (2 marks)

A: x = (3ab + b√(9a² − 8))/2 or x = (3ab − b√(9a² − 8))/2.

Q: Solve (x + 4)/(x + 3) = (2x − 5)/(x − 3), where x ≠ −3, 3. (2 marks)

A: x = √3 or x = −√3.

Q: Find k for which x² − 2(k + 2)x + k² = 0 has equal roots. (2 marks)

A: k = −1.

Q: The product of two consecutive positive integers is 306. Form a quadratic equation to find the integers. (2 marks)

A: x² + x − 306 = 0. The integers are 17 and 18.

Q: Determine whether x² − 2√3x + 1 = 0 has real roots. If it does, find them. (2 marks)

A: Two distinct real roots: x = √3 + √2 or x = √3 − √2.

Q: Solve 15/x − 1 = 12/(x − 2), where x ≠ 0, 2. (3 marks)

A: No real roots.

Q: Solve 1/(x − 4) + 2/(x − 1) = 5/3, where x ≠ 1, 4. (3 marks)

A: x = (17 + 3√6)/5 or x = (17 − 3√6)/5.

Q: Divide 18 into two parts such that the square of the larger part exceeds the square of the smaller part by 180. (3 marks)

A: 14 and 4.

Q: The perimeter of a right-angled triangle is 60 cm and its hypotenuse is 25 cm. Find the area of the triangle. (4 marks)

A: Area = 150 cm².

Q: A square garden has a side of 40 m. A uniform path is constructed around the garden. The total area of the garden and the path is 2304 m². Find the width of the path. (4 marks)

A: 4 m.

Q: Is it possible to design a rectangular park with a perimeter of 84 m and an area of 432 m²? If so, find its length and breadth. (4 marks)

A: Yes. Length = 24 m and breadth = 18 m.

## Worksheet 3

A: D = 0.

A: D = 16 − 4k.

A: D = 109.

Q: Solve 6x² + 13x + 6 = 0 by factorisation. (2 marks)

A: x = −2/3 or x = −3/2.

Q: Solve 3x² + 2x − 8 = 0 by factorisation. (2 marks)

A: x = 4/3 or x = −2.

Q: The product of two consecutive positive integers is 306. Form a quadratic equation to find the integers. (2 marks)

A: x² + x − 306 = 0. The integers are 17 and 18.

Q: Find the value(s) of k for which x² + kx + 25 = 0 has equal roots. (2 marks)

A: k = 10 or k = −10.

Q: Solve x + 4/x = 5, where x ≠ 0. (2 marks)

A: x = 1 or x = 4.

Q: Solve 18x − 30/x = 27. (3 marks)

A: x = (9 + √321)/12 or x = (9 − √321)/12.

Q: Solve 1/(x − 2) − 1/(x + 4) = 3/5, where x ≠ 2, −4. (3 marks)

A: x = −1 + √19 or x = −1 − √19.

Q: Solve 7x + 3/x = 35/3, where x ≠ 0. (3 marks)

A: x = (35 + √469)/42 or x = (35 − √469)/42.

Q: Determine whether 4x² + 5√2x − 8 = 0 has real roots. If it does, find the roots. (3 marks)

A: Yes. x = (−5√2 + √178)/8 or x = (−5√2 − √178)/8.

Q: The sum of the squares of two consecutive positive integers is 365. Find the two integers. (4 marks)

A: 13 and 14.

Q: A train travels 360 km at a uniform speed. If its speed is increased by 5 km/h, it takes 48 minutes less. Find the original speed. (4 marks)

A: 45 km/h.

Q: The length of a rectangle is 10 cm more than its breadth. If its area is 240 cm², find its dimensions. (4 marks)

A: Breadth = (√265 − 5) cm and length = (√265 + 5) cm.
