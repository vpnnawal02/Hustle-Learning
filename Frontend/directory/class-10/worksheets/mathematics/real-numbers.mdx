## Worksheet 2

Q: How many positive factors does a prime number have? (1 mark)

A: 2.

Q: State the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic. (1 mark)

A: Every composite number can be expressed as a product of primes, and this factorisation is unique, apart from the order of the factors.

Q: Find the exponent of 3 in the prime factorisation of 216. (1 mark)

A: 3, since 216 = 2³ × 3³.

Q: Find the sum of all the exponents appearing in the prime factorisation of 150. (1 mark)

A: 4, since 150 = 2 × 3 × 5².

Q: Find the HCF of the smallest composite number and the smallest odd prime number. (1 mark)

A: 1, since HCF(4, 3) = 1.

Q: State whether the following is True or False: the HCF of two positive integers is always a divisor of their LCM. (1 mark)

A: True.

Q: Express 120 as a product of its prime factors. (2 marks)

A: 2³ × 3 × 5.

Q: Express 455 as a product of its prime factors. (2 marks)

A: 5 × 7 × 13.

Q: The HCF of 324 and 540 is 108. Find their LCM. (2 marks)

A: 1620.

Q: Two positive integers are a = x⁴y² and b = x²y⁵, where x and y are distinct primes. Find LCM(a, b). (2 marks)

A: x⁴y⁵.

Q: If HCF(420, 588) = 84, find LCM(420, 588). (2 marks)

A: 2940.

Q: Three walkers step 72 cm, 84 cm and 96 cm. What is the shortest distance each must walk to complete it in an exact number of steps? (3 marks)

A: 2016 cm, that is, 20.16 m.

Q: Three people step 32 cm, 40 cm and 48 cm. Find the minimum distance each should walk in whole steps. (3 marks)

A: 480 cm, that is, 4.8 m.

Q: Traffic signals change every 45, 60 and 75 seconds. If all change together at 8:00 AM, when do they next change simultaneously? (3 marks)

A: 8:15 AM.

Q: Find the greatest six-digit number exactly divisible by 18, 24 and 30. (3 marks)

A: 999720.

Q: Two numbers have an HCF of 18 and an LCM of 360. Is it possible for one of the numbers to be 72? Justify. (4 marks)

A: Yes. The other number is 90, since 18 × 360 / 72 = 90, and HCF(72, 90) = 18 with LCM(72, 90) = 360.

Q: The HCF of two numbers is 135 and their LCM is 2700. If one number is 675, find the other. (4 marks)

A: 540.

Q: Find the smallest positive integer which, when increased by 13, becomes exactly divisible by both 480 and 720. (4 marks)

A: 1427.
