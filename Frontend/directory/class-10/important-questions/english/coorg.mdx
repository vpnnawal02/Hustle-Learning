### Section A: Very Short Answer Questions (1-2 Marks)

Q: Where is Coorg situated?

A: Coorg, also known as Kodagu, is the smallest district of Karnataka. It is situated midway between Mysore and the coastal town of Mangalore.

Q: Why is Coorg described as a piece of heaven?

A: Coorg is described as a piece of heaven because of its rolling hills, evergreen rainforests, coffee plantations, spices, rich wildlife and pleasant weather.

Q: When is the best time to visit Coorg?

A: The best time to visit Coorg is from September to March, when the weather is pleasant and suitable for sightseeing and outdoor activities.

Q: What is Coorg famous for?

A: Coorg is famous for its evergreen rainforests, coffee plantations, spices, scenic hills, wildlife, adventure sports and brave, hospitable people.

Q: What are the two theories regarding the origin of the Coorgi people?

A: One theory suggests that the Coorgi people are descendants of Greek soldiers from Alexander's army, while the other suggests that they have Arabic ancestry.

Q: What is a kuppia?

A: A kuppia is a long, black coat with an embroidered waist-belt traditionally worn by the Kodavus. It resembles the kuffia worn by Arabs and Kurds.

Q: Why is the Coorg Regiment famous?

A: The Coorg Regiment is one of the most decorated regiments in the Indian Army. The people of Coorg are renowned for their bravery and martial traditions.

Q: Who was General Cariappa?

A: General Cariappa was the first Indian Chief of the Indian Army and belonged to Coorg.

Q: Which river obtains its water from the hills and forests of Coorg?

A: The River Kaveri obtains its water from the hills and forests of Coorg.

Q: Name any four adventure activities available in Coorg.

A: Four adventure activities available in Coorg are river rafting, canoeing, rappelling and rock climbing. Visitors can also enjoy mountain biking and trekking.

Q: What is Nisargadhama?

A: Nisargadhama is a sixty-four-acre island that visitors can reach by walking across a rope bridge.

Q: What is the significance of Bylakuppe?

A: Bylakuppe is home to India's largest Tibetan settlement. Its Buddhist monks, dressed in red, ochre and yellow robes, are an important cultural attraction for visitors to Coorg.

### Section B: Short Answer Questions (3 Marks)

Q: Describe the natural beauty of Coorg.

A: Coorg is a picturesque region of Karnataka characterised by rolling hills, evergreen rainforests, coffee plantations and spice gardens. The air is filled with the refreshing aroma of coffee, while mist-covered hills create a beautiful landscape. The region is also rich in wildlife, including elephants, kingfishers, squirrels and langurs. Its rivers, forests and pleasant weather make it an ideal destination for nature lovers.

Q: What are the possible origins of the Coorgi people, and what evidence supports these theories?

A: There are two theories regarding the origin of the Coorgi people. According to the first, they may be descendants of Greek soldiers from Alexander's army who settled in the region when returning became difficult. They married local people, and their distinct customs are believed to reflect this ancestry. The second theory suggests Arabic origins, supported by the traditional kuppia, a long, black coat resembling the kuffia worn by Arabs and Kurds.

Q: What qualities make the people of Coorg unique?

A: The people of Coorg are known for their bravery, independence, hospitality and strong martial traditions. They take pride in the achievements of their sons and fathers and often recount stories of courage. Their association with the Indian Army, particularly the distinguished Coorg Regiment, reflects their military heritage. Their distinctive customs and traditional dress further distinguish them from other communities.

Q: How does the author establish the martial tradition of the Coorgi people?

A: The author highlights the martial tradition of the Coorgi people by mentioning the distinguished Coorg Regiment, one of the most decorated regiments in the Indian Army. General Cariappa, the first Indian Chief of the Indian Army, was also from Coorg. The author further emphasises their reputation for bravery and notes the special privilege traditionally associated with carrying firearms without a licence.

Q: Describe the wildlife found in Coorg.

A: Coorg supports a rich variety of wildlife because of its forests, hills and rivers. The River Kaveri contains mahaseer, a large freshwater fish, while kingfishers dive into the water to catch their prey. Squirrels and langurs drop partially eaten fruit into the river, creating splashes. Elephants can also be seen enjoying baths in the river. Macaques, Malabar squirrels and slender lorises inhabit the tree canopies, adding to the region's biodiversity.

Q: How does Coorg cater to the interests of adventure enthusiasts?

A: Coorg offers a variety of activities for adventure enthusiasts. Visitors can enjoy river rafting and canoeing in its waterways, as well as rappelling and rock climbing in its hilly terrain. Mountain biking and trekking along the numerous walking trails provide further opportunities for exploration. The climb to the Brahmagiri hills rewards visitors with panoramic views of the mist-covered landscape, making Coorg an attractive destination for adventure lovers.

Q: Why does the author advise visitors to be cautious around wild elephants?

A: Coorg is home to wild elephants that inhabit its forests and natural surroundings. Although the author describes the region's wildlife enthusiastically, the statement about stepping aside for wild elephants reflects an awareness of their strength and unpredictable behaviour. It suggests that visitors should admire wildlife from a safe distance and respect the natural habitat of wild animals.

Q: What makes Coorg a place of cultural diversity?

A: Coorg combines its distinctive local traditions with the cultural influence of other communities. Its people are known for their unique martial customs, traditional clothing and hospitality. The nearby Tibetan settlement at Bylakuppe adds another cultural dimension to the region. Visitors can encounter Buddhist monks dressed in red, ochre and yellow robes, making Coorg a destination where natural beauty and cultural diversity coexist.

Q: How does the River Kaveri contribute to the natural environment of Coorg?

A: The River Kaveri receives its water from the hills and forests of Coorg and plays an important role in sustaining the region's ecosystem. Its waters support aquatic life, including mahaseer fish, while birds such as kingfishers depend on the river for food. Animals such as squirrels and langurs also interact with the river environment. The river adds to Coorg's scenic beauty and provides opportunities for water-based adventure activities.

Q: Why is September to March considered the ideal period to visit Coorg?

A: September to March is considered the ideal period to visit Coorg because the weather is pleasant and suitable for outdoor activities. The monsoon season, which brings heavy rainfall, has largely passed, making the region more accessible to visitors. During these months, tourists can explore coffee plantations, enjoy trekking, participate in adventure sports and admire the scenic beauty of the hills and forests.
