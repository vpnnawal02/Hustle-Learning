Life processes — nutrition, respiration, transportation and excretion — keep every living organism alive. This chapter compares how plants and animals carry them out.

## Worked examples

Q: Why is diffusion insufficient for multicellular organisms? {demo}

A: In large multicellular bodies, cells are far from the surface and have higher demands, so specialised transport systems (xylem/phloem in plants, blood circulation in animals) are needed beyond simple diffusion.

Q: How does transpiration help plants? {demo}

A: Transpiration creates a suction pull that draws water and dissolved minerals upward through the xylem, and it also cools the plant on hot days.
