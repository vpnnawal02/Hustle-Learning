## Stanza 1

```stanza
The way a crow
Shook down on me
The dust of snow
From a hemlock tree
```

**Explanation:** The poet describes how a crow sitting on a hemlock tree shakes its branches, causing a small amount of snow to fall on him. The snow falls like fine particles of dust and touches the poet. A crow and a hemlock tree are generally associated with negativity because the crow is often considered an unpleasant bird, while the hemlock is a poisonous tree. However, this ordinary incident becomes significant because it changes the poet's mood.

**In simple words:** A crow shakes snow off a hemlock tree, and the falling snow lands on the poet.

## Stanza 2

```stanza
Has given my heart
A change of mood
And saved some part
Of a day I had rued
```

**Explanation:** The poet explains that the falling snow changes his mood and makes him feel better. Before this incident, he was unhappy and regretted how his day had been going. However, the unexpected touch of the snow refreshes his mind and helps him overcome some of his sadness. As a result, at least a part of what seemed like a bad day becomes worthwhile.

**In simple words:** The falling snow changes the poet's mood and saves a part of his otherwise unhappy day.

## Important Literary Devices

| Literary Device | Example | Explanation |
| --------------- | ------- | ----------- |
| Symbolism | Crow and hemlock tree | They traditionally represent negativity, sadness or unpleasantness. |
| Imagery | The dust of snow | Creates a visual image of fine snow particles falling from a tree. |
| Alliteration | Has given my heart | The repetition of the initial h sound creates a musical effect. |
| Enjambment | The way a crow / Shook down on me | A sentence continues from one line to the next without a pause. |
| Rhyme scheme | ABAB CDCD | The poem follows a regular rhyme pattern. |
